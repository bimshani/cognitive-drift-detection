Before you begin

A short check-in about how your studies are going. Please read this once.

What this is. You'll answer a few quick questions, write a few sentences, and if you like record a short voice note. A computer-based system reads your responses and gives you a supportive note, and it looks for early signs of academic stress. It is not a medical or clinical tool and it does not give a diagnosis.

Who can see your responses. You, and the counsellor who registered you. Your check-ins and the results are stored securely and are used to support you. They are not shared with anyone else.

Your choice. Taking part is voluntary and the voice note is optional. You can stop at any time and ask your counsellor about your data.

If you're struggling. This check-in is not an emergency service. If you ever feel unsafe or in distress, please contact the KDU Student Counselling Unit or someone you trust right away.

Please note: write and speak in English, since the analysis currently supports English only.
